# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in CORTEX
and its community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments directed at individuals
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Using this project's tools against systems you do not own or have
  explicit written permission to test

## Scope

This project exists for authorized security research, penetration testing
education, and CTF practice. Contributions or discussion promoting
unauthorized access to systems will be removed.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported by opening an issue or
contacting a maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed.
